How to Play Boat Ride 2.0

Goal

Complete all 4 challenges — Boat School, Boat Store, Catch Fish, Rescues — then reach WIN with a positive balance. Pass START to collect $5!

1. Boat School

Earn 3 stars (2 with Shortcut). Graduate before buying a boat.

2. Boat Store

Buy a boat once graduated.

3. Catch Fish

Collect 10 fish (6 with Shortcut).

4. Rescues

Perform 2 rescues (1 with Shortcut).

Shipwreck Cards — $10 each, 1 per turn. Cards are secret — only you see yours!
Debt: 5% interest per turn. Sacrifice a challenge to reset to $0.
